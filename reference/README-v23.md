# One Grain — reference v23 (sound)

`v23-sound.html` is the spec for Phase 5. Everything outside the sound section matches
v15. Port only the sound engine and the sound button behaviour.

The sound is fully synthesised (Web Audio, no files):
- music beneath: one chord per chapter, crossfaded pads, sparse soft notes, a three-note
  motif on arrival, a soft repeated note in industry;
- ambience in front: per-world events, a babbling river with a soft water rush,
  prompt coast waves;
- complete silence at the "One day," cut and after the hero grain lands.
