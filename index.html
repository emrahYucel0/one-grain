One Grain

Nature

Magma

Deep underground, molten rock cools very slowly. Inside it, a quartz crystal begins to grow.

Granite

Over millions of years the rock is pushed upward. The crystal is now part of a mountain.

River

Frost cracks the rock and sets it free. The river carries it downstream, tumbling and chipping as it goes.

Coast

Waves throw it onto the beach and pull it back, over and over.

Desert

Wind drives it inland. Bouncing across the dunes, it is worn round and frosted.

Move your cursor across the dunes.

Tap the dunes.

Again

Buried, pressed into stone, lifted and worn away, again and again, until it settles in a bed of almost pure quartz.

Industry

Quarry

One day, a machine digs that bed open.

Furnace

In an arc furnace far hotter than lava, carbon pulls the oxygen away. What is left is silicon.

Purity

It is purified again and again, until fewer than one atom in a billion is anything else.

Crystal

From the melt, one flawless crystal is slowly pulled upward and turned. The grain is part of a crystal again.

Wafer

The crystal itself is cut, into discs about as thick as a credit card, then polished to a mirror.

Light

On one of those discs, patterns are printed with light, layer upon layer.

Now

Chip

Now the grain is one of billions of tiny switches, wired in perfect order.

Move your cursor over the circuit.

Tap the circuit.

Glass

Their signals become light, and the light passes through glass. The glass is sand too.

You are looking at sand right now.
